LexiQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

LexiQuest (“we”, “the app”) is a vocabulary learning application. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to our servers. The app works offline. Study progress is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app may save locally on your phone or tablet:

  • XP, level, and daily goals
  • Word mastery status (new, learning, known)
  • Recent study history
  • Notification preference (on/off)

This data stays in app storage (localStorage). Uninstalling the app removes it.

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, or account (unless you add sign-in in a future version)
  • Location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Analytics or advertising IDs (in the current version)

Third-party services

The current version does not send your vocabulary progress to external servers. Built-in word hints are generated on-device. If a future update adds optional cloud features, this policy will be updated.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation uses your device’s system speech engine. Audio is processed locally by your operating system.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences learning vocabulary. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Clear app data: Android Settings → Apps → LexiQuest → Storage → Clear data
  • Remove all data: uninstall the app

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
